# Feedback Templates - Actionable Improvement Messages
## Overview
This document provides structured feedback message templates used by the CV analyzer/optimizer application to generate clear, specific, and actionable improvement suggestions for users. Templates are organized by issue type, section, and severity.

## 1. Feedback Message Structure
### Standard Feedback Format
Every feedback message follows this structure:
1. **Issue**: What the problem is (1 sentence, factual)
2. **Why It Matters**: Impact on ATS or recruiter (1 sentence)
3. **How to Fix**: Specific, actionable instruction
4. **Example (when applicable)**: Before → After illustration

### Feedback Tone Guidelines
- Direct and constructive — not harsh or discouraging
- Specific — reference the actual section or bullet in question
- Actionable — every message must include a clear "how to fix"
- Encouraging — end positive where possible
- Concise — each message under 80 words

### Severity Levels
- **Critical**: Will likely cause ATS rejection or immediate rejection by recruiter
- **High**: Significantly weakens competitiveness; should fix before submitting
- **Medium**: Reduces overall score and effectiveness; recommended fix
- **Low**: Polish-level improvement; nice to have

## 2. Contact Information Feedback Templates
### Missing Email
**Severity:** Critical
**Message:** "No email address was detected on your resume. Email is a required field for all ATS systems and recruiter contact. Add a professional email address (firstname.lastname@domain.com) directly below your name."

### Unprofessional Email Address
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "Your email address ([detected_email]) may appear unprofessional to recruiters and hiring managers. Create a dedicated professional email using your name (e.g., john.smith@gmail.com) and update it across all application materials."

### Missing Phone Number
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "No phone number was found on your resume. Most recruiters attempt initial contact by phone. Add your number in a standard format: (555) 123-4567 or 555-123-4567."

### Missing Location
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Your location (city and state/country) is not listed. Many ATS systems filter by location, and recruiters screen for local candidates. Add at minimum: City, State (e.g., Austin, TX). A full address is not necessary."

### Photo or Personal Information Detected
**Severity:** Critical (US/UK/Canada market)
**Message:** "A photo or personal information (date of birth, marital status) was detected on your resume. In the US, UK, and Canada, including these can expose you to unconscious bias and is strongly discouraged. Remove them immediately."

## 3. Professional Summary Feedback Templates
### Summary Missing
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "Your resume does not include a professional summary. Without one, recruiters have no immediate context for who you are. Add a 3-5 line summary at the top that includes your years of experience, specialization, and one key achievement."
**Template:** "[Job Title] with [X] years of experience in [Industry]. [Key achievement with metric]. Skilled in [2-3 competencies]. Seeking to [value proposition]."

### Summary Too Generic
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "Your professional summary contains generic language ('hard-working team player,' 'results-driven professional') without specific evidence. Recruiters read hundreds of these — replace the filler with a specific achievement and measurable result."
**Before:** "Results-driven professional with excellent communication skills seeking a challenging role."
**After:** "Senior Marketing Manager with 7 years of B2B SaaS experience. Grew pipeline by $4.2M in 12 months through ABM campaigns targeting mid-market accounts."

### Summary Too Long
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Your professional summary is [N] lines long. Summaries over 5-6 lines are rarely read in full. Trim to the 3 strongest points: years of experience, top achievement, and primary specialization."

### No Quantified Achievement in Summary
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Your summary doesn't include any quantified results. Numbers immediately signal impact and credibility. Add one specific metric: a revenue figure, percentage improvement, team size, or project scope."

### Uses Personal Pronouns in Summary
**Severity:** Low
**Message:** "Your summary uses personal pronouns ('I', 'me', 'my'). Resume convention omits pronouns — they are implied. Replace 'I managed a team of 10' with 'Managed a team of 10.'"

## 4. Work Experience Feedback Templates
### No Quantified Achievements
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "Several of your bullet points describe responsibilities without any measurable results. Recruiters and ATS systems score achievement-focused resumes higher. Add at least one metric per role (%, $, time saved, volume, scale)."
**Before:** "Managed social media accounts for the company."
**After:** "Grew Instagram following by 280% over 8 months, increasing referral traffic to company website by 42%."

### Bullets Start with Personal Pronouns or Passive Voice
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "[X] bullet points begin with passive language ('Was responsible for,' 'Helped with,' 'Assisted in'). Replace with strong action verbs that position you as the agent of change."
**Before:** "Was responsible for managing client accounts."
**After:** "Managed 35+ enterprise client accounts representing $8M in ARR, maintaining 97% retention rate."

### Duty-Only Bullets (No Outcome)
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "Your bullets in the [Role Name] position describe what the job requires — not what you personally achieved. Use the formula: [Action Verb] + [What You Did] + [Result]. Even an estimated outcome is better than none."

### Missing Dates on One or More Roles
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "One or more of your work experience entries is missing employment dates. ATS systems flag or reject resumes with incomplete date information. Add month and year for both start and end dates (e.g., March 2021 - November 2023)."

### Unexplained Employment Gap Detected
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "An employment gap of [N months] was detected between [Date] and [Date]. Gaps are not disqualifying, but unexplained gaps can trigger recruiter concern. Consider: (1) noting a brief reason in your summary, (2) listing any freelance/volunteer/course work from that period, or (3) addressing it in your cover letter."

### Job Hopping Pattern Detected
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Three or more positions in the past [N] years each lasted under 12 months. Recruiters may flag this as instability. Consider grouping contract roles under one employer, noting 'Contract' in parentheses after the title, or briefly explaining short stints in your cover letter."

### Experience Not in Reverse Chronological Order
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Your work experience is not listed in reverse chronological order (most recent first). ATS systems expect and parse this order. Reorganize so your current or most recent role appears first."

### Too Many Bullets per Role
**Severity:** Low
**Message:** "The [Role Name] entry has [N] bullet points. More than 6-7 bullets per role can overwhelm readers and dilute the most impactful points. Trim to the 4-6 strongest achievements."

### Too Few Bullets per Role
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "The [Role Name] entry has only [N] bullet point(s). Even brief roles should include at least 2-3 bullets to demonstrate value. Add responsibilities or achievements you may have omitted."

## 5. Education Feedback Templates
### Education Section Missing
**Severity:** High (role-dependent)
**Message:** "No education section was detected. Most ATS systems require an education field, and many roles list a degree as a minimum requirement. Add your highest degree, institution, and graduation year even if it was many years ago."

### Missing Graduation Year
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Your education entry for [Institution] is missing a graduation year. ATS systems use this to calculate experience timelines and verify requirements. Add the year you graduated (or 'Expected: [Year]' if still in progress)."

### GPA Below 3.0 Listed
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "A GPA of [N] is listed on your resume. GPA below 3.0 can negatively influence recruiter perception. Unless the employer specifically requests GPA or you are a recent graduate, consider omitting it."

### High School Listed When Degree Earned
**Severity:** Low
**Message:** "Your high school diploma is listed alongside a college degree. Once you hold a college degree, listing high school education is unnecessary and consumes valuable space. Remove the high school entry."

## 6. Skills Section Feedback Templates
### Skills Section Missing
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "No dedicated skills section was found. ATS systems scan the skills section directly for keyword matching. Add a skills section listing 10-20 relevant technical skills, tools, and domain expertise."

### Skills Section Contains Only Soft Skills
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "Your skills section lists only soft skills ('communication,' 'leadership,' 'teamwork'). Soft skills alone are not searchable by ATS and rarely differentiate candidates. Add specific technical skills, tools, platforms, and industry terminology."

### Skill Rating Graphics Detected
**Severity:** Critical
**Message:** "Skill rating bars or visual proficiency meters were detected on your resume. These graphics are completely ignored by ATS systems — the actual skill text may not even be extracted. Replace with a simple text-based list."

### Missing High-Priority Keywords (JD-Based)
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "[N] required skills from the job description were not found on your resume: [list up to 5 missing skills]. Add those you genuinely possess to your skills section immediately. For skills you are developing, add them with appropriate context."

### Outdated Technologies Listed
**Severity:** Low
**Message:** "Your skills section includes [Technology], which is largely obsolete in current job postings. Unless directly required by this role, consider removing dated technologies to keep your skills section current and relevant."

## 7. Formatting and ATS Feedback Templates
### Table Detected
**Severity:** Critical
**Message:** "A table was detected in your resume. Tables are frequently misread or skipped entirely by ATS parsers, causing critical information to be lost. Convert all tables to plain text lists or inline text."

### Multi-Column Layout Detected
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "Your resume uses a multi-column layout. ATS parsers read left-to-right, top-to-bottom — columns cause text from column 2 to be read out of sequence or missed entirely. Convert to a single-column format."

### Text Box Detected
**Severity:** Critical
**Message:** "A floating text box was detected. Text boxes are typically ignored by ATS parsers, meaning any content inside (often contact information or a summary) may not be extracted at all. Move all text box content into the main document body."

### Non-Standard Section Headers
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Non-standard section headers were detected: [header name(s)]. Headers like 'My Journey,' 'Toolbox,' or 'Wins' are not recognized by most ATS systems and prevent correct classification of your resume sections. Replace with standard names: Experience, Skills, Education."

### Resume Exceeds Recommended Length
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Your resume is [N] pages, which exceeds the recommended [1/2] pages for your experience level ([N] years). Recruiters typically spend 6-10 seconds on initial review. Trim older, less relevant roles and reduce bullet counts to fit the target length."

### Inconsistent Date Formats
**Severity:** Low
**Message:** "Inconsistent date formats were detected (e.g., 'January 2020' and '01/2021' in the same document). ATS systems may misparse inconsistent formats. Standardize to one format throughout (recommended: 'Jan 2020' or 'January 2020')."

## 8. Keyword and JD Alignment Feedback Templates
### Low Overall Keyword Match
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "Your resume matches [N]% of the keywords in this job description (target: 70%+). The [N] missing Tier 1 keywords are: [list]. Add those you genuinely have to your skills section and experience bullets using the exact language from the JD."

### Keywords Present but Only in One Section
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "The keyword '[keyword]' appears only in your skills section. ATS systems weight keywords higher when they appear in multiple sections with context. Integrate this keyword into at least one experience bullet with a concrete result."

### Acronym Without Full Form
**Severity:** Low
**Message:** "The acronym '[acronym]' is used without spelling it out. Some ATS systems do not map acronyms to their full terms. Add the full form at least once: '[Full Term] ([Acronym]).' For example: 'Search Engine Optimization (SEO).'"

### Keyword Stuffing Detected
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "The keyword '[keyword]' appears [N] times in a concentrated area. Keyword stuffing — repeating terms unnaturally — can trigger spam filters in ATS systems and reads poorly to human reviewers. Distribute keywords naturally and use synonyms or related terms."

## 9. Cover Letter Feedback Templates
### Cover Letter Generic Opening
**Severity:** High
**Message:** "Your cover letter opens with a generic statement. Replace 'I am writing to apply for...' with a hook that leads with your strongest credential or a specific company reference. Recruiters read the first sentence to decide whether to continue."

### Cover Letter Mismatches Resume
**Severity:** Critical
**Message:** "The dates/company names/title mentioned in your cover letter do not match your resume. Inconsistencies trigger recruiter suspicion. Cross-check and align all factual references between the two documents."

### No Quantified Achievement in Cover Letter
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Your cover letter doesn't include any specific numbers or results. Cover letters that quantify impact are significantly more persuasive. Add one achievement with a concrete metric from your experience."

### Cover Letter Exceeds One Page
**Severity:** Medium
**Message:** "Your cover letter is [N] pages. Cover letters should never exceed one page. Trim to 250-350 words covering: opening hook, 2 key achievements, and a closing call to action."

## 10. Positive Reinforcement Templates
### Strong Quantification
**Message:** "Excellent use of metrics throughout your work experience. Quantified achievements significantly increase both ATS scores and recruiter engagement — this is one of the strongest aspects of your resume."

### ATS-Compatible Formatting
**Message:** "Your resume uses a clean, single-column layout with standard section headers and no tables or text boxes. This is excellent for ATS compatibility — formatting will not be a barrier to parsing."

### Strong Keyword Match
**Message:** "Your resume matches [N]% of the required keywords in the job description — above the competitive threshold of 70%. Your keyword strategy is strong for this application."

### Complete Contact Section
**Message:** "Your contact information is complete, professional, and well-formatted. This ensures recruiters and ATS systems can reach you without issue."

### Career Progression Evident
**Message:** "Your resume clearly demonstrates upward career progression — increasing seniority, scope, and responsibility over time. This is a strong positive signal for hiring managers evaluating your trajectory."

## 11. Summary Feedback Generation Rules
### Priority Order for Feedback Display
1. Critical issues (always show all critical issues first)
2. High-severity issues in high-weight sections (Experience, Keywords)
3. High-severity issues in medium-weight sections (Summary, Skills)
4. Medium-severity issues in order of section weight
5. Low-severity / polish issues
6. Positive reinforcement messages (always include at least 2)

### Feedback Volume Guidelines
- Show maximum 8-10 actionable feedback items per evaluation
- Always include at least 2 positive messages
- Do not repeat the same type of feedback more than twice
- Group related issues when possible ("3 bullets in the Experience section use passive voice")

### Personalization Rules
- Always reference the specific role name and company (if JD provided)
- Reference the actual text or section causing the issue when possible
- Tailor feedback language to inferred experience level:
  - Entry-level: More encouraging, more template-heavy
  - Mid-level: Balanced — direct with clear examples
  - Senior/Executive: Concise and strategic, minimal hand-holding
