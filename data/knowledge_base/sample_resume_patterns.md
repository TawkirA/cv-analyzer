# Sample Resume Patterns - Templates and Examples
## Overview
Collection of proven resume patterns, structures, and examples across different experience levels, industries, and situations.
## 1. Resume Structure Patterns

- Linear career progression
- Consistent work history

- Traditional industries
- Most professionals
